# 🎓 Key Business Features

EduTrack covers the complete academic lifecycle — from student admission through to final results — for educational institutions of all sizes.

---

## 📚 Student Management

- **Student Lifecycle** — Admission, enrollment, academic progression, and graduation tracking
- **Profile Management** — Personal details, contact information, emergency contacts, and academic history
- **Enrollment Workflows** — Prerequisite validation, capacity limits, waitlist management, and drop/add rules
- **Attendance Tracking** — Individual and bulk attendance recording with status (Present, Absent, Late, Excused) and correction workflows

---

## 🏫 Academic Structure

- **Department & Program Management** — Hierarchical academic organization (Faculty → Department → Program → Course)
- **Course Catalog** — Rich course metadata, credit hours, grading scale, prerequisites, and capacity configuration
- **Course Scheduling** — Session planning, instructor assignment, classroom booking, and conflict detection
- **Course Lifecycle** — Draft → Active → Completed with enrollment period configuration

---

## 👨‍🏫 Faculty Management

- **Teacher Profiles** — Academic credentials, employment history, specializations, and professional development records
- **Academic Titles** — Lecturer, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Full Professor with promotion workflows
- **Workload Management** — Course assignment validation, capacity planning, and teaching load distribution
- **Employment Status** — Active, On Leave, Terminated with corresponding workflow automation

---

## 📊 Grades & Assessments

- **Flexible Grading Schemes** — Letter grades, GPA scales, percentage-based, or pass/fail per course
- **Assessment Types** — Quizzes, assignments, mid-terms, finals with configurable weightings
- **Grade Processing** — Calculation, validation, and publication workflows
- **Transcript Generation** — Official academic transcripts and grade reports

---

## 🔐 Access Control

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** — Admin, Registrar, Faculty, Student, Parent roles
- **Dynamic Permissions** — Granular permission assignments per role or per user
- **Multi-Tenancy Ready** — Isolated data per institution or campus

---

## 📈 Reporting & Analytics

- **Academic Dashboards** — Enrollment numbers, grade distributions, attendance rates
- **Custom Reports** — Filterable reports by term, department, course, or student cohort
- **Export Capabilities** — PDF, Excel, CSV output for all major reports
